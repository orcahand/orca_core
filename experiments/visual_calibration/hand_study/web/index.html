What exactly is optimised

Parameters. The 17 joint angles (URDF limits as bounds: MCP −25…100°, PIP/DIP −15…107°, abductions about ±27°, wrist −65…35°, thumb CMC −45…33°, thumb ABD −18…55°). The hand's mount on the plate (3 rotations, 3 translations) is fixed to the baseline: solved once together with the joints from 15 views of the zero pose (fit 8.2 px). The camera, lens and plate angles come from the rig calibration (1a/1b); each photo's plate angle is the turntable read-back.

Views. All photos of the pose (sparse run: plate 45, 112.5, 180, 225, 292.5°; pilot: 0…315° every 45°), at 960 px wide (the 3840 px photo / 4). The cost is summed over all views.

Mask. Per view: GrabCut (OpenCV, 3 iterations, colour Gaussian mixtures learned from this photo, no fixed threshold or stored background) on a crop of the model's box + 40 px. Seeds: “probably hand” within 5 px of the model rendered at the current estimate (first round: orca_core's assumed angles; second round: the first round's result), “probably background” elsewhere, “surely background” beyond 35 px of the model and on the crop border. Then a 3×3 opening. Note the seed comes from the model, so a wrong start can pull the mask; panel 3 shows where it happened.

Edges. Canny (thresholds 30/90) on the blurred grey image, kept only within 2 px of the mask.

Cost (squared residuals, px at 960 width):
1. inside: 60 surface points per link (240 on palm and tower) projected; each residual = distance to the mask (0 inside), capped at 12 px. Keeps the model inside the hand.
2. cover: 3000 mask pixels; each residual = distance to the model silhouette, capped at 12 px. Makes the model cover the hand.
3. edges: 600 edge pixels; each residual = 0.7 × distance to the nearest model outline (silhouette or the border between two links, nearest link in front), capped at 4 px. Pins the fingers' outlines and the folds between phalanges.
4. priors (n = number of image residuals): joints 0.02·√n·angle/30° (barely there), and against over-extension of the flexion joints 0.5·√n·min(angle,0)/30°.

Search. Two rounds, each: per finger a grid over its two flexion joints (MCP −20…80, PIP −10…90, 20° steps; the others held), then bounded Levenberg–Marquardt with a numeric Jacobian (12 iterations, 1.5° steps). Quality numbers: “fit px” = RMS of the inside + cover residuals in 3840-px pixels; the split solves the pose again from the even and the odd views alone, and their difference is the per-joint noise used on this page.

Suspect masks (excluded from every graph): a view where over 15 % of the mask is not explained by the model at the solution, or the mask misses over 25 % of the model's fingers.

Error per joint

1 · Motor position → joint angle

Each panel is one joint. Top: joint angle against motor position: measured points, the orca_core linear map (the line between the two hard stops found by calibration), and the configured joint limits (dashed). The ◆ markers are where the joint actually ended up when commanded to a configured limit, i.e. the real hard stops. Bottom: residual (measured − assumed) against motor position. A flat residual at 0 means the linear map is right. A slope means a wrong gain, an offset means a wrong zero, and a curve means the joint is not linear in the motor.

2 · Cross-joint dependencies

Partial correlation between a joint's error (row) and another joint's measured angle (column), with the part explained by the row joint's own angle removed. Blue = error grows with the other joint, red = shrinks; ● marks |t| ≥ 3 (significant). Coupling through shared tendon paths (e.g. the wrist, or MCP → PIP) shows up here.

3 · Repeatability and hysteresis

The same commanded pose visited several times, approached from below (−15°) and from above (+15°). The spread is the noise floor any calibration can reach. The above − below difference is hysteresis (tendon friction, backlash) that a static lookup table cannot remove.

4 · Vision quality

How far to trust a measured difference. Split = |estimate from the even views − estimate from the odd views| (4 photos each); σ = the fit's 1-σ from its Jacobian. Differences between the measured and assumed angles smaller than these are not real.

5 · Lookup-table calibration

Fitted once enough data is collected: a commanded → true map per joint with cross-joint terms, checked on held-out poses.
